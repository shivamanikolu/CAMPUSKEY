BREADBOARD THE BLUE AND RED CONNECTION:
• The Ground Bus (Blue Rail Highway): A single wire runs from a GND pin on the NodeMCU directly into the long side rail marked with a blue line. The grounds of the LCD, RFID, Buzzer, and both LED resistors plug cleanly into this shared row.

• The 3.3V Bus (Red Rail Highway): A single wire runs from a 3V3 pin on the NodeMCU into the long side rail marked with a red line. This distributes safe 3.3V operating voltage to both the 3.3V power input and the RST reset line of the RC522 RFID reader simultaneously.
